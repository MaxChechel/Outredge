<CaseSection id="brief" number="01" title="Brief">

<Lede>Replit needed an online presence for Vibecon that felt as premium as the event itself — editorial, fast, and technically refined. The design was heavy on media assets and large typographic forms, with motion and scroll behavior central to the experience.</Lede>

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection id="solution" number="02" title="Solution">

We built the page in Webflow with GSAP animations for scroll-driven reveals, entrance sequences, and stat counters, each calibrated to the editorial layout rather than applied as a blanket style.

With rich visual assets and full-bleed media driving the aesthetic, performance needed active attention. Custom loading strategies and structural optimizations kept the experience fast despite the media-heavy design.

<Clip src="replit-vibecon-1" aspect="16/9" label="Vibecon landing page media collage" />

The invite form was the technically nuanced part of the project. It dynamically adapted its copy based on user input, creating a more conversational feel. After submission, the experience transitioned into an infinite scroll flow, extending engagement rather than ending it abruptly.

<Clip src="replit-vibecon-2" aspect="16/9" label="Vibecon signup form, with oversized animated field labels" />

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection id="result" number="03" title="Result">

A landing page that feels more like a product than a marketing page. The performance architecture, motion quality, and custom form experience combine to make it the kind of digital presence a flagship event actually deserves.

</CaseSection>
