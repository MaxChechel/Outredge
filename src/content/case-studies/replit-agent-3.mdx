<CaseSection id="brief" number="01" title="Brief">

<Lede>For the launch of Agent 3, Replit needed a product page fast, one that matched the energy of the release and carried a new visual identity built specifically for this product. We worked directly with their marketing team and design agency to deliver it in a matter of a week.</Lede>

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection id="solution" number="02" title="Solution">

One of the core branding concepts was fluid line movement. We built a hero reveal sequence that sets the tone immediately, then carried that motion language throughout the page using scroll-based SVG transitions between sections.

<Clip src="replit-agent-3-2" aspect="3/2" label="Replit Agent 3 hero, with the animated orange ribbon" />

A key branded moment was a custom typewriter-style animation with a pixelated reveal effect, not achievable with standard Webflow interactions. Built with GSAP and custom JavaScript, it became one of the more distinctive visual details of the launch.

<Clip src="replit-agent-3-3" aspect="3/2" label="Replit Agent 3 introduction sequence, with pixelated type transitions" />

The design called for multiple sliders with different reveal animations per instance. We integrated Swiper JS and layered GSAP on top for full control over each transition. Replit produced a large volume of video assets for the launch, we engineered a dedicated loading and playback system to keep performance smooth across the whole page.

<Clip src="replit-agent-3-4" aspect="3/2" label="Replit Agent 3 feature slider moving between product screenshots" />

</CaseSection>

<CaseSection id="result" number="03" title="Result">

Launched on schedule despite the compressed timeline. The page delivered the dynamic, product-specific feel Replit needed for a major release, technically solid under the hood, visually sharp on the surface.
